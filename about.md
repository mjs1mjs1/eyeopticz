<p class="lead">This website explores specific aspects of human vision, including topics that have rarely been studied. It is run by Mike Simpson, Ph.D., who currently has a separate website for consulting work [simpsonoptics.com](https://simpsonoptics.com).

</p>

## Research interests

- Intraocular lenses (IOLs)
- The nodal point and the optics of the wide-angle eye
- Far peripheral vision and how intraocular lenses change it
- Negative and positive dysphotopsia
- Focal length and the definitions used in physiological optics
- Intraocular lens tilt and "angle alpha"
- The history of physiological optics
