<p class="lead">Many of the original books and papers that founded the optics of the eye are now online. This page links to the ones about paraxial optics, the nodal point and focal length, with a short note on each.</p>

Good places to find scanned originals, where copyright allows, are [Google Books](https://books.google.com), [archive.org](https://archive.org) and [Forgotten Books](http://www.forgottenbooks.org).

## Original publications on geometrical optics and the eye

### Gauss (1841), *Dioptrische Untersuchungen*
The foundation of paraxial optics and the cardinal points.
[Original German](https://books.google.com/books?id=9K8IwX26fVwC) · [Commentary in Taylor's *Scientific Memoirs* (1843)](https://books.google.com/books?id=qsY-AAAAYAAJ&pg=PA490) · [English translation (PDF)](http://17centurymaths.com/contents/gauss/Dioptrische%20UntersuchungenTr.pdf)

### Listing (1845), *Beitrag zur physiologischen Optik*
Introduced the nodal point for the eye.
[Original German, Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/fvazxph9) · [1905 reprint, Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33093362M/Beitrag_zur_physiologischen_Optik) · [English translation by M. J. Simpson]({{ '/papers/listing-1845-translation/' | relative_url }})

### Helmholtz, *Treatise on Physiological Optics*
Three volumes; volume 1 was translated into English in 1924.
[Volume 1, Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=1EzPAAAAMAAJ) · [Biographical article](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/0964704X.2021.1904182)

### Donders (1864)
[*On the Anomalies of Accommodation and Refraction of the Eye*, Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=UexhAAAAcAAJ)

### Moessard (1889)
An earlier description of the nodal-slide method for finding nodal points.
[Gallica](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k9602140r/f136.item.zoom)

### Clay (1904, 1911)
The "nodal slide" test for lenses.
[1904 article](https://www.google.com/books/edition/The_Photo_beacon/erkaAAAAYAAJ?gbpv=1&pg=PA109) · [*Treatise on Practical Light* (1911)](https://archive.org/details/treatiseonpracti00clayuoft)

### Beck (1903, 1905, 1907)
The optical bench, equivalent planes and the microscope.
[Lens testing (1903)](https://digi.ub.uni-heidelberg.de/diglit/jbpr1903/0271) · [Equivalent planes (1905)](https://books.google.com/books?id=RzLOAAAAMAAJ&pg=PA9) · [1907 lecture](https://books.google.com/books?id=261JAAAAYAAJ&pg=PA22) · [*The Microscope*](https://archive.org/details/microscope02beckuoft) · [Beck and Andrews, *Photographic Lenses*](https://archive.org/details/photographiclensOObeck)

### Glazebrook (1905)
Inaugural address at the London Optical Convention.
[Google Books](https://books.google.com/books?id=RzLOAAAAMAAJ&pg=PA1)

### London Optical Convention (1912)
Includes an address by Silvanus Thompson and a paper by G. F. C. Searle, author of *Experimental Optics* (1925).
[Proceedings, archive.org](https://archive.org/details/proceedingsofopt00optirich)
