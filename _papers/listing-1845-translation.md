Johann Benedict Listing's *Beitrag zur physiologischen Optik* (1845) introduced the nodal point for the eye. This is an English translation, shared so that the original reasoning can be read by people who do not read German. The original German text is at the [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works/fvazxph9).
